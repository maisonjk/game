Privacy Policy

Social Levels · Effective November 4, 2026

The short version: Social Levels doesn’t have accounts, doesn’t show ads, doesn’t track you, and doesn’t collect your personal information. Your game progress lives on your device.

Who we are

Social Levels is made by Jiae Kim (“we,” “us”). Questions: innerlink24@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The nickname you type, your archetype, level, aura, stars, streak, badges, and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app, or tapping Settings → Reset progress, erases them.

Daily reminders

If you turn on the optional Daily Chaos reminder, the app schedules notifications on your phone using iOS. Nothing is sent to us or any server, and no push token is created. Turn it off anytime in the app (You → Settings) or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or the choices you make in the game. There is no sign-up, no chat with other players, and no user-generated content shared with anyone. We don’t use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and we don’t sell or share data.

In-app purchase

If you buy “Unlock All Worlds,” payment is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. To confirm the purchase and let you restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app identifier, your purchase receipt from Apple, basic device details (such as iOS version and country/storefront), and the app version. This information is used only to deliver and restore your purchase. It is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Kids and teens

Social Levels is designed for players 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you’re a parent or guardian and have a question, email us and we’ll help.

Your choices and rights

Because we don’t hold personal information about you, there’s nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete. You control your data on your device. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us and we’ll request deletion of the anonymous purchase record on your behalf. Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws have rights to know, delete, and opt out of sale or sharing. We don’t sell or share personal information.

Security

Game data is stored with your device’s standard app storage. Purchase data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for meaningful changes, note it in the app’s update notes.
