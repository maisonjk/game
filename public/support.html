How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Me tab → Settings → Restore purchase, using the same Apple ID you bought with.

I got a new phone. Where’s my progress?

Progress is saved only on the device you play on, so it doesn’t move to a new phone. Your purchase does: just tap Restore purchase.

How do I start over?

Me → Settings → Reset progress. Your purchase stays unlocked.

How does Daily Chaos work?

A new quick scenario drops every day at midnight on your phone’s clock. Play it on back-to-back days to grow your streak.

How do refunds work?

Apple handles all payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Need to talk to someone?
Social Levels is a game, not therapy. If you’re going through something hard, talk to a parent, school counselor, or another adult you trust. In the U.S., call or text 988 anytime, or text HOME to 741741. If anyone is in danger, call 911.
